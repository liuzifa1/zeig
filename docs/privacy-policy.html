Zeig! Support

Privacy Policy

Zeig! doesn't collect, sell, share or track any data. Your files stay on your device.

Summary

There are no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs, and the app never connects to the internet. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Your files

  • Archives are opened and created on your device. Files never leave it unless you share them yourself.
  • Files you import are copied into Zeig!'s own folder (On My iPhone › Zeig! in the Files app). Deleting the app deletes that folder.

Stored on this device

  • Your settings and the Recent list. Clear Recent from the + menu on the Recent tab.
  • Passwords you type are used for that one operation and never stored.

This data is part of your device backup like any other app's, and is removed when you delete the app.

Permissions

Zeig! doesn't ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or local network. It only reads files you pick or open in it.

Open-source components

Zeig! uses 7-Zip, libarchive and other open-source libraries to read and write archives. They run inside the app, on your device, and send nothing anywhere. The full list is in Settings › Open Source Acknowledgements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub, or see the Support page.
